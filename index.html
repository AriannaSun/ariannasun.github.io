About Me

Arianna Sun is a high school senior at Forsyth Central High School. Since she was 8, Sun has been drawing and painting. She works with various mediums like pastel, oil, encaustics, acrylic, and fabrics. Sun uses her art as a mode of communication, exploring themes of cultural and generational identity.
